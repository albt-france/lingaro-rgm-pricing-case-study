## 1. Lingaro public website
- Pages read by `src/collect_lingaro.py`, at a slow rate (2 s between requests):
  - home and About pages;
  - AI at Lingaro;
  - Marketing & Commercial Analytics;
  - Data Visualization;
  - case studies, including Brand Guardian;
  - the CPG price elasticity / RGM case study;
  - articles on price elasticity, promotion uplift, price reductions and CPG revenue leakage;
  - the ISG and Everest Group recognitions;
  - Great Place to Work 2024;
  - the careers page and the RGM Data Analyst posting.
- Each fact in `data/public/lingaro_facts.csv` has its URL and retrieval date.
- Company figures are Lingaro's own statements (for example 1,800+ employees, 75% of Fortune 200 CPG companies); they were not independently verified.

## 2. dunnhumby "Breakfast at the Frat"
- Weekly sales over 156 weeks from 2009 to 2012 for 4 categories: cold cereal, bag snacks (pretzels), frozen pizza, oral hygiene (mouthwash). The stores are in several US states.
- One row per week x store x product. Columns:
  - units, visits, households, spend;
  - price paid and base price;
  - feature (circular), display (in-store display), TPR only (temporary price reduction).
- Lookups:
  - products: manufacturer, category, sub-category, pack size;
  - stores: city, state, segment, size.
- **How to get it**:
  1. Fill in the free form on dunnhumby's Source Files page.
  2. Put `dunnhumby_Breakfast-at-the-Frat.zip` in `data/raw/retail/`.
  3. Run the pipeline (see README).
- **What is published here**:
  - aggregated results: elasticities, promotion tables, price-pack table, quality counts;
  - no row-level data.
- dunnhumby's own notice describes the data as replicating real in-store patterns. It is a retailer's data from the US, from 2009 to 2012. It is used to demonstrate the method, not to describe today's European market.

## 3. Eurostat HICP
- Annual average rate of change and average index (2015 = 100), 2019-2025, for:
  - geographies: EU27, DE, FR, IT, ES, PL, NL;
  - COICOP groups: all items (CP00), food (CP011), bread & cereals (CP0111), sugar/jam/confectionery (CP0118), personal care products (CP1213).
- Monthly annual rate of change for all items and food.
- Retrieved through the Eurostat dissemination API by `src/collect_eurostat.py`. Raw JSON answers are kept in `data/raw/eurostat/`.
