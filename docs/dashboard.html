How it works

Volume = (1+p)e; revenue = (1+p)1+e; gross profit = (1+p)e x (m+p)/m. Revenue rises with price only if |e| < 1. Gross profit rises with a small increase while |e| < 1/m (3.3 at a 30% margin).

The model assumes a constant elasticity near observed prices, no competitor or retailer reaction, and an assumed margin: a first-order estimate for a pricing proposal, not a forecast.

The elasticity is observational (a correlation on historical prices), from a log-log regression with feature, display, store x product and month effects and a trend; errors clustered by store.

Promotion effectiveness

Uplift = promo-week units vs a baseline of clean non-promo weeks within ±8 weeks. ROI = (profit with the promotion - profit without it) / discount cost, at an ASSUMED margin. Feature and display fees are not in the data, so their ROI is optimistic.
